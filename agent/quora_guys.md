# Quora Watch — Guys definitions

Formulaic sockpuppet-troll archetypes ("guys"). Each defined type below is a standing Red trigger — a profile matching most of a type's traits is an immediate Red: block it, then extract up to 100 of its followers, assess each individually, and block the matches. Do not expand to the followers' followers. (The 100-follower sweep is an explicit exception to the per-Red follower setting.)

## Defined guy types

- "Guys" (Billy Flowers archetype — added): a sockpuppet-troll profile matching most of these: (1) joke/provocative display name with fake, absurd employment credentials or date ranges (e.g. "...1947–present"), joke or offensive education/location entries; (2) internally inconsistent bio vs. credentials — thin, slapped-together identity; (3) content nearly all absurdist shock-humor (formulaic absurd hypotheticals, gross-out jokes, recurring joke-character series, punchline one-liner answers, or incoherent pseudo-intellectual jargon-salad questions fired at machine cadence — added), zero informational content; (4) burst cadence — long dormancy punctuated by clusters of actions minutes apart, occasional self-deleted items; (5) tiny engagement (low-hundreds of views, single-digit followers); (6) social graph of sibling joke-identity accounts with the same fake-credential style, not real users. A match is an immediate Red — extract up to 100 of the guy's followers first (explicit exception to the max_followers_per_red=20 scan setting — the 100 stands for guy-type sweeps), assess each individually, then block the matches (subject to the block cap in force); do NOT expand to the followers' followers (: assess and block followers, no recursion).
