# Quora Watch — Guys definitions

Formulaic sockpuppet-troll archetypes ("guys"). Each defined type below is a standing Red trigger — a profile matching most of a type's traits is an immediate Red: block it, then extract up to 100 of its followers, assess each individually, and block the matches. Do not expand to the followers' followers. (The 100-follower sweep is an explicit exception to the per-Red follower setting.)

## Defined guy types

- "Guys" (Billy Flowers archetype — added): a sockpuppet-troll profile matching most of these: (1) joke/provocative display name with fake, absurd employment credentials or date ranges (e.g. "...1947–present"), joke or offensive education/location entries; (2) internally inconsistent bio vs. credentials — thin, slapped-together identity; (3) content nearly all absurdist shock-humor (formulaic absurd hypotheticals, gross-out jokes, recurring joke-character series, punchline one-liner answers), zero informational content; (4) burst cadence — long dormancy punctuated by clusters of actions minutes apart, occasional self-deleted items; (5) tiny engagement (low-hundreds of views, single-digit followers); (6) social graph of sibling joke-identity accounts with the same fake-credential style, not real users. A match is an immediate Red — block. Then extract up to 100 of the guy's followers (explicit exception to the max_followers_per_red=20 scan setting — the 100 stands for guy-type sweeps), assess each individually, and block the matches; do NOT expand to the followers' followers (: assess and block followers, no recursion).
- "Leftoid" (polemic-guy type — added): a sockpuppet-troll profile matching most of these: (1) provocative joke-style display name built around a named ideological enemy ("Anti-Islamo-Leftoid" pattern — the account brands itself as an attacker of a political out-group rather than as a person); (2) thin, slapped-together identity: unverifiable formulaic credentials ("Independent Researcher 2015–present" style), bio stating a polemical mission rather than a personal identity, no real-photo avatar, no credible employment/education/location; (3) burst cadence — brand-new account with all content dumped in a short window; (4) tiny engagement (0–few followers, near-zero views); (5) content that is partisan polemic or mocking attack-framing of the named out-group (loaded bait questions, mocking joke questions) rather than informational content. The verdict rests on the sockpuppet-troll pattern — fake identity + burst bait + named-enemy branding — never on political viewpoint alone: a real person with a real identity writing partisan polemic stays clear. A match is an immediate Red — block. Then extract up to 100 of the Leftoid's followers (explicit exception to the max_followers_per_red=20 scan setting — the 100 stands for guy-type sweeps), assess each individually, and block the matches; do NOT expand to the followers' followers (same as the Guys rule).
- "Musical Theater" (template-spam guy type — added): a sockpuppet-troll profile matching most of these: (1) thin, slapped-together identity — no bio or generic bio, default avatar, plain/generic display name; (2) a single rigid question template repeated dozens of times with only the title swapped (the observed template: "If [movie/TV series/episode] got a stage musical, what songs would you write for it? What would the plot be and what would be different from the movie?"); (3) burst cadence — brand-new account with all content dumped in short windows; (4) zero answers and zero posts — pure question spam; (5) tiny engagement (single-digit followers, 0–2 answers per question); (6) the template applied mechanically to incongruous subjects (horror movies, real-world atrocities, fictional-sounding titles). Note: the archetype is mechanical template repetition about stage musicals, NOT theatrical roleplay, lyric-quoting, or camp character performance. The verdict rests on the mechanical template-repetition + burst spam pattern, never on an interest in musical theater alone: a real theater fan writing varied, genuine questions stays clear. A match is an immediate Red — block. Then extract up to 100 of the guy's followers (explicit exception to the max_followers_per_red=20 scan setting — the 100 stands for guy-type sweeps), assess each individually, and block the matches; do NOT expand to the followers' followers (same as the Guys rule).
- "streets of" (pity-bait guy type — added): a sockpuppet-troll profile matching most of these: (1) thin, slapped-together identity — no bio, default avatar, plain/generic display name, brand-new account; (2) formulaic first-person homeless-pity-bait questions built on a fixed "streets" template (observed: "How can I [verb phrase] while living and sleeping on the streets in Ontario with absolutely no money at all, no income, no ID, and no belongings?") with only the opening verb phrase swapped between variants; (3) copy-paste repetition — near-identical long tails across questions; (4) often paired with a sexualized childhood-memory shock-bait question; (5) zero answers and zero posts of its own, tiny or zero engagement. The verdict rests on the mechanical template-repetition + pity-bait sockpuppet pattern, never on homelessness alone: a real person asking genuine questions about homelessness stays clear. A match is an immediate Red — block. Then extract up to 100 of the guy's followers (explicit exception to the max_followers_per_red=20 scan setting — the 100 stands for guy-type sweeps), assess each individually, and block the matches; do NOT expand to the followers' followers (same as the Guys rule).
